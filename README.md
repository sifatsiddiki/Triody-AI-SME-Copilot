# Triody-AI-SME-Copilot
AI finance copilot for small businesses. Forecasts 60 days of cash with risk ranges, alerts the owner before a shortfall, explains the cause, and matches financing options it can prove are affordable. Drafts applications, never moves money. Topic E entry, 2026 Shenzhen FinTechathon International Track. Triody combines Tri, for our team of three and the three tasks of our agent, with Ody, from Odyssey. A small business runs a long journey, and our copilot travels with the owner. The three tasks are cash-flow alerts, financing-option matching, and business financial analysis.
