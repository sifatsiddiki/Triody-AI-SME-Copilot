Triody AI is a finance agent for small business owners (Topic E). It takes part in the business's money decisions instead of waiting to be asked. Each day it forecasts cash for the next 60 days with a probability range, and when the balance is likely to fall under a safety buffer it warns the owner with the date, the size of the gap and the cause, such as a VAT bill. It analyses margins, cost trends, the biggest suppliers and break-even sales. It compares financing options built from public sources, ranks them with transparent rules, refuses anything the business cannot afford, and re-runs its own forecast to show how the best option changes the risk. It prepares the paperwork, but the owner approves every action with a one-time code sent on a separate channel, money transfers are blocked in code, and every step goes into a tamper-evident log. A language model can plan the steps, with a rule-based fallback. One catalogue file sets the country: we show a UK café and a Shenzhen tea shop, all synthetic.
